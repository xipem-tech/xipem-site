Monlings Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what data the Monlings mobile game ("the app") collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

We are the data controller for the data described here. Contact: support@xipem.com. This address is also our contact for data protection requests (including as the LGPD "encarregado" channel).

The short version

  • No accounts. Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app makes a random install ID when you first open it. It is not your name, email or phone number.
  • We use Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, Google Firebase for analytics and crash reports, and our own server for game settings and to check in-app purchases.
  • Personalised ads and ad tracking happen only if you allow them (Apple's tracking prompt, and the consent form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • You can delete the data linked to your install ID from Settings > Delete my data.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details. Apple processes payments; we only receive a signed record of the purchase.

Consent, tracking and ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you see Google's consent form (UMP). Until you answer, analytics and ad storage stay off. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Apple tracking prompt (ATT): if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the app does not access your IDFA and ads are not personalised with it. Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising ID either.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch an ad, and no reward depends on allowing tracking.
  • We ask Google to show only ads rated for general audiences (G / PG).

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users (for example in a fraud case).

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Brazil or California) you have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

How to use them:

  • Delete my data: Settings > Delete my data deletes the purchase records linked to your install ID on our server and resets your Firebase Analytics ID, so new events are no longer linked to old ones (past analytics events expire after 2 months). Coins already granted stay in your game.
  • Ad choices: Settings > Ad privacy options (consent form), and iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Anything else: email support@xipem.com with the install ID shown in Settings, so we can find your data. We answer within 30 days (15 days for LGPD requests where required).

Because we have no accounts, the install ID is the only way to find your data. If you delete the app, a new install gets a new ID and we can no longer link the old data to you; it is then deleted on the schedule above.

Children

Monlings is a general-audience game rated 9+ on the App Store. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), we do not knowingly collect personal data from them, and we ask Google not to treat the app as child-directed. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact support@xipem.com and we will delete it.

Security

The app talks to our server only over HTTPS. Purchase records are checked against Apple's signature before we store them. Our server keeps no passwords or payment details because none are collected.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
